🚗 Car Rental

🚗
One-Way Rental: Kagoshima → Fukuoka
Pick up Kagoshima-Chuo area · Drop off Fukuoka Airport or city · One-way fee applies (~¥5,000–15,000 extra) · Book in advance

Required for Singapore licence holders · Get at SingPost or AA Singapore BEFORE travelling
